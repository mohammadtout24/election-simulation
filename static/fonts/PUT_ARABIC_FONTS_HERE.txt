Put one of these font pairs in this folder:

Option A (recommended mixed Arabic + English):
- DejaVuSans.ttf
- DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf

Option B:
- NotoSansArabic-Regular.ttf
- NotoSansArabic-Bold.ttf

Do not rename the files differently unless you also update app.py.
The PDF exporter checks this folder first:
static/fonts/
